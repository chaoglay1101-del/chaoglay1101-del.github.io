Research, coursework, and practice

  1. Final-year research · 2025–2026

    Digital payment pressure and bank net fee margins

    A panel of 39 commercial banks in Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand, 2014–2022. The log specification shows a negative, statistically significant pooled association that comes mainly from differences between countries: a conditional association, not a causal effect.

    A five-dashboard Tableau story built from World Bank Global Findex data. Its finding that digital payment use runs ahead of debit-card ownership raised the question the final-year research then tested.
